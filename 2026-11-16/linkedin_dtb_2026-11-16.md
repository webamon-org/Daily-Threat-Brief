## ⭐ New campaign spotlight — fake TV scandal investment fraud

We've added one new tracked campaign this window: [Central Bank Governor Fake TV Scandal Investment Lure](https://intel.webamon.com/campaigns/1817e9b37bcd7891dd69cd369f6776c6da49aabe) — 69 domains, first seen 19 June 2026, active today.

It's a fake-news investment fraud lure localised per country: a central bank governor or finance chief storms off a live TV show (BBC One, BBC Breakfast, Question Time UK; RTE One Ireland; M6 France; 10 vor 10 Switzerland; Tercer Grado Mexico; La Noche de Mirtha and TN Argentina; Ecuador) after being accused of robbing families, with the article funnelling readers into an investment platform. The operators use throwaway no-token domains and per-country builds, some cloaking to a benign news page. Found on 16 November while prepping a central-bank CTI call — 69 domains across 8 countries at promote, and 24 of 27 rechecked were still hosting.
